📐 The 75% Rule

Most colleges require 75% attendance. For every 4 classes, you can skip 1 and stay safe.

🧮 How It Works

Attendance % = (Attended ÷ Total) × 100. The bunk limit is the max you can miss while staying above the threshold.

⚡ Pro Tip

Track each subject separately — some professors are stricter. Keep a 5–10% buffer above the requirement.
